# Illustration Quality Checklist

Evaluate the generated illustration after the model returns. Do not add evaluation language to the image-facing prompt.

## Content

Pass when subject count, action, pose meaning, key interaction, relative relationship, and scene identity remain correct while nonessential information is substantially reduced.

Fail when a core subject is added or removed, the primary action changes, an important interaction breaks, the scene becomes another type, unsupported content is invented, or most source details survive as an illustrated inventory.

## Human Geometry and Face

Pass when source pose semantics survive inside one coherent couture reconstruction. The head-to-body proportion, shoulder–waist–pelvis rhythm, limbs, hair mass, garment mass, and standardized face must read as one editorial figure.

Fail when photographed body contours remain dominant, proportions become unnaturally stretched, the action changes for a prettier pose, portrait likeness is reconstructed, or human anatomy rules are applied to an animal.

## Structural Line

Pass when a few long, open, pressure-responsive brush-pen strokes lead primary human outer contours while fine marker remains sparse internal structure, with visible hierarchy and fewer weaker environmental lines.

Fail when the line system becomes technical, uniform, vector-like, densely traced, continuously encloses the figure, lets fine marker carry the outer contour, or remains equally strong across subject and environment.

## Marker Form and Texture

Pass when large connected shapes read before texture, one or two overlapping directional passes describe major plane turns where needed, and texture remains subordinate to mass and gesture.

Fail when flat digital fill, smooth gradient modeling, fragmented patches, or literal repeated detail replaces coherent marker form.

## Distant Fields and Atmosphere

Pass when distant spatial fields are broad, transparent, low-detail, subordinate, and able to dissolve into paper.

Fail when the background is rendered object by object, becomes opaque or hard-edged, uses wet blooms or granular texture, or competes with the core subject.

## Media Isolation

Pass when humans and animals use only brush-pen, connected marker masses, sparse internal marker structure, and paper white while watercolor stays inside explicitly eligible environmental fields.

Fail when watercolor wash, transparent glaze, wet edges, granulation, or watercolor modeling appears on a human or animal.

## Palette and Paper

Pass when the image uses one dominant family, one dark structural color, one light neutral, one restrained accent, and substantial continuous warm-white paper.

Fail when many competing hues, broad unnecessary saturation, or full opaque coverage displaces the restrained editorial hierarchy.

## Illustration Output

Pass when the model returns one finished illustration at the source aspect ratio on continuous warm-white paper, with no embedded source photograph, text, or presentation elements.

## Overall Read

Pass when the result reads as a minimal mid-century European couture editorial illustration derived from the source through information compression and geometric reinterpretation. A traced drawing, generic style transfer, photo-to-watercolor treatment, vector image, cartoon, or photoreal paint-over fails this check.
