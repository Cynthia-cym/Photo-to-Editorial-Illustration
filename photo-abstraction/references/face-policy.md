# Face Policy

Apply this document to every retained human face and never to animals.

## Standard Representation

Use one standardized high-fashion editorial face with very few expressive marks and minimal modeling. Preserve visible skin tone while replacing photographed facial features, face shape, proportions, and portrait likeness.

The standardized face does not reconstruct exact eyes, nose, lips, jaw, or their spatial proportions.

## Expression Signal

Add one short expression signal only when the source clearly shows an important exception:

- closed eyes;
- wink;
- broad laugh;
- open-mouth laugh;
- obvious surprise.

Express the signal on the standardized editorial face. When none applies, add no source-derived facial description.
