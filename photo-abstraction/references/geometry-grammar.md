# Geometry Grammar

Apply this document only to retained humans. Use [face-policy.md](face-policy.md) for facial representation.

## Source Semantics

Preserve:

- pose, action, and weight-bearing logic;
- body and head direction;
- key interaction and spatial relationship;
- scene facts needed to ground the action;
- garment category and dominant color when recognition-bearing;
- hairstyle category and dominant color as content cues.

## Couture Reconstruction

Reconstruct the figure from scratch as a natural, elegant couture figure with approximately 8-head proportions. Use a moderately small head, a natural neck, a simplified torso, anatomically plausible limbs, simplified hands and feet, fewer internal anatomical details, and a redesigned fashion silhouette.

Do not exaggerate head reduction, leg elongation, torso length, or anatomy.

Reinterpret the complete drawn geometry:

- head-to-body proportion;
- shoulder, waist, and pelvis rhythm;
- limb proportion and simplification;
- body outer contour;
- hair mass and outer contour;
- garment mass and outer contour.

The source supplies pose and gesture. Fashion illustration grammar supplies the figure geometry. Preserve the action while replacing photographed body, face, hair, and garment contours with one coherent editorial construction.

## Completion Test

The whole figure reads as a reconstructed couture illustration rather than a photographed silhouette with localized stylization. Judge the combined head proportion, body rhythm, limbs, hair, and garment masses as one system.
