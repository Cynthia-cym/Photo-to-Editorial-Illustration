# Content Abstraction Grammar

Use this document to decide which source information survives. Geometry, medium, and presentation belong to their dedicated owners.

## Internal Distillation

Assign source information to four internal roles without exposing the analysis:

- **ANCHOR — exactly one:** the primary fact carrying recognition, action, relationship, or the visual center.
- **SUPPORT — one or two:** secondary masses establishing place, depth, relationship, or rhythm.
- **CUE — only a few:** indispensable axes, directions, overlaps, intervals, or rhythmic signals.
- **DROP — everything else by default:** low-information, repetitive, decorative, incidental, or redundant content.

Derive every role from the current photograph rather than an object-category or named-scene template.

## Preservation Boundary

Preserve the semantic facts needed to recognize the source:

- number and role of core subjects;
- primary pose, gesture, action, and interaction;
- relative position and depth of core subjects;
- scene identity at the minimum useful level;
- dominant color relationships;
- a small number of identity-bearing recognition cues.

## Abstraction Operations

Apply the operations that best compress each retained element:

- **OMIT:** remove low-value, repetitive, or decorative detail.
- **SIMPLIFY:** reduce internal structure.
- **COMPRESS:** express complex information with fewer visual units.
- **MERGE:** combine repeated small elements.
- **GENERALIZE:** replace precise local shapes with larger representative forms.
- **OPEN CONTOUR:** leave boundaries incomplete where recognition survives.
- **REDUCE PALETTE:** merge nearby colors into fewer families.
- **REDUCE DENSITY:** lower environmental and textural information density.
- **SELECTIVE EMPHASIS:** strengthen only recognition, pose, relationship, or rhythm.

## Completion Test

The result remains recognizably derived from the source while carrying substantially less visual information. Individually preserving most source objects, boundaries, textures, or surface details means the distillation is incomplete.
